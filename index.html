About these notes

This is not a technology blog and a philosophy blog awkwardly sharing a URL. The two subjects are connected. The systems I build are increasingly capable of changing, measuring, predicting, and controlling the world. The philosophical question is what kind of judgment we need alongside that capability.

I want to write from inside the technical world rather than from outside it: learn the machine carefully, then ask what assumptions made the machine seem necessary in the first place.
