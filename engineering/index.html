What this section is for

These are engineering notes rather than marketing copy. The goal is to explain what changed, what was measured, what surprised me, and which mental model survived contact with the tools. The HLS work here follows the hackathon’s fixed flow: describe the network, train it, convert it to fixed point, synthesize with HLS, measure time and power, and reason from energy as power × time.
